I'm Jennifer, I do research at a film university, and this is my second game jam ever. The first one ended with a free VR headset and no game. This one is the **Epic AI MegaJam 2026**: four days, Unreal Engine, AI everywhere.

This devlog is written while it happens. Three prep entries cover the time *before* the jam: the setup, the night of testing everything, and the push and pull of not having a concept yet. One entry per jam day follows, and a post-mortem closes it. Every entry opens with a recap of what existed before it, because with agents that build in minutes, the line between "testing my tools" and "already making the game" gets thin. I'd rather show it than blur it.

The words are mine, dictated at odd hours. AI helped condense them and pulled the timestamps out of git and the agent logs. You can tell which is which: dictated passages are highlighted.

The little dragon is Funke. It lives in a terminal pane next to my code and guards my scope.
