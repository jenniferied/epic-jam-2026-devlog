I'm Jennifer Meier. I work in **ProSys OSVP** at [Filmuniversität Babelsberg KONRAD WOLF](https://www.filmuniversitaet.de), a publicly funded research project on production support systems for on-set virtual production: the kind of film set where an LED wall and a game engine take the place of the green screen.

I didn't start in film or in games. I studied interior and environmental design at Duncan of Jordanstone College of Art and Design in Dundee, and that is where I learned to think in spaces. Everything since has been about building those spaces digitally: VR showrooms and real-time apps in Glasgow, digital twins of machine tools, then more than four years at TH OWL, teaching virtual film production and looking after motion capture, camera tracking and borrowed LED walls.

My master's thesis asked how far one person gets with AI tools in the pre-production of a fantasy role-playing game. The answer was: surprisingly far, and not without a human who curates. That question hasn't left. This jam is the same question with a deadline.

## research focus

- **Virtual production education.** How LED-volume workflows are taught, what a university can do on a small budget, and what the literature actually knows about it.
- **Mapping a young field.** Scoping reviews and bibliometric evidence maps of virtual production research.
- **AI in game development.** Image generation and multi-agent workflows in pre-production, tested in practice instead of in pitch decks.
- **Generative agents and living worlds.** What it takes for an open RPG sandbox to become a simulated world with consequence and memory, where playing and making fall together.
